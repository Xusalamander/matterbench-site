The System

MatterBench 是什么WHAT IT IS

MatterBench 是 OriginFab 正在开发的软硬件一体化创意制造系统，把 AI 辅助设计软件和桌面制作设备结合起来，帮助设计师、创客及教育用户，把想法变成可组装、可调整的实体作品与功能原型。

你在软件里说出需求、比较并修改方案，再把构件做出来，完成组装、测试与迭代。它面向日常物件的创作、改造与功能验证，也可以加上传感器和驱动模块，变成能感知、会响应的交互原型。

MatterBench is an integrated creative fabrication system under development at OriginFab. It combines AI-assisted design software with a desktop making device, helping designers, makers and educators turn ideas into physical creations and functional prototypes that can be assembled and modified.

Express what you need, explore and refine designs, then make the parts to assemble, test and iterate. It supports everyday creation, adaptation and functional prototyping, and sensors and actuators can extend creations into interactive prototypes that sense and respond.

  5. 传感与驱动拓展Sense and respond

Try It Online

三关在线体验TRY THE DEMO · 3 LEVELS

从简单到有挑战:① 照着图案拼一张拼豆像素画;② 做一个发光灯罩,给里面的 ESP32 + RGB 灯板选灯效,带走能直接烧录的代码;③ 用豆条拼一个会动的小人,豆子上的孔就是关节,摆姿势、让它动起来。不用注册,全部在本地运行。

Three short levels: trace a bead pixel picture, light up a bead lampshade driven by an ESP32 and RGB panels (and take the code with you), then build a moving bead-strip figure whose joints are the bead holes. No sign-up; everything runs in your browser.

关于 OriginFabABOUT US

我们是一支来自设计、人机交互与数字制造领域的小团队，正在打磨 MatterBench 的原型。我们相信，做东西的人应该能把想法直接变成可以上手试、可以反复改的实物。
